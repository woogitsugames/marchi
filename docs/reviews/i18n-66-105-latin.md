**Total changed: 317 strings.**

Recurring fixes across languages:
- **Articles/agreement in front of good placeholders.** Goods are bare nouns of mixed gender
  and number (de Holz/Bretter, es troncos/tablas/pan, fr bûches/pain …). Forms like
  „die {goodAcc}“, „las {goodAcc}“, „les {goodGen}“, „poco {goodGen}“, „es wartet“ broke for
  some goods; rephrased with case/gender-neutral constructions (`castle.rival`,
  `fix.paceHaulWhy`, `fix.surplus`, `fix.rankWhy`, `seal.tempo.producer`, `neck.why`).
- **"Build another: {name}"** assumed a masculine/common-gender building; made neutral
  (fr/it/pt_BR/sv/de).
- **Term consistency** inside each language: stall (es atasco, pt paralisação, tr
  duraklama, de Stillstand), road = the Road tool's word (de Weg), castle (nl kasteel, sv
  slott in UI), rank (pt nível), renown (fr renommée, tr itibar), pace (fr rythme, tr hız),
  valley (nl vallei), good (es/pt mercadoria).
- **XIII title** „Pierwsze piętro łańcucha“: the literal "floor" was replaced by the
  idiomatic chain *link* (es eslabón, fr maillon, it anello, pt elo, sv länk, tr halka);
  de „Stufe“ and nl „trede“ kept (natural). **„marchia“** in `menu.m13Sub`: sv „gränsmark“,
  nl „grensmark“ (plain „mark“ reads as ground/currency); de „Mark“, tr „uç beyliği“,
  Romance „marca/marche/marca“ kept.
- **Order 66 pitfall** de „alle # Wagen fahren“ → „alle # Wagen unterwegs“; `fix.rankWhy`
  de fixed (pronoun agreement). Plural ICU branches without a verb completed (it, pt_BR).
- **Turkish:** no case suffixes glued to placeholders (`{by} saatine kadar`, `istemiyor`),
  polite imperative in tutorial/help to match `tutorial.m1.*`.
- **Length:** order-card titles, playtest buttons and card titles over ~1.3× en shortened
  (e.g. de „Markttische“, fr „Goulot“, it „Strozzatura“, pt „Meio a meio“).

Known remaining over-length (kept on purpose, natural term with no shorter equivalent):
de `pin.worst` (1.55), es `hud.bottleneck` „Cuello de botella“ (1.70), tr
`events.order.t.fair_tables` (1.64). Visual check is left to the coordinator's
`npm run layout`.
