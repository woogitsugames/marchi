**Total changed: 132 strings** (+1 outside the 207: ar `why.inbound`, order 100).

Recurring fixes:
- **Order 105, ru/uk case after „ради/заради {aim}“.** {aim} is a nominative numeral phrase („3 печати“), so
  „ради“ (+ genitive) broke for every number. Rephrased as „Чтобы получить {aim} к {by}, …“ / „Щоб отримати
  {aim} до {by}, …“ (accusative = nominative for inanimate nouns, correct for 1/3/5 seals).
- **ru/uk numerals without a noun** in `cause.paceFleet` / `cause.paceHaul` („все # в пути“): noun added per CLDR
  category (повозка/повозки/повозок, віз/вози/возів), plus a `one` branch for 21, 31 … next to `=1`.
- **Gender of building names.** {who}/{name} are buildings of both genders (ru Лесопилка f., hi झोंपड़ी f.,
  ar المنشرة f.). Masculine-only predicates („{who} один снабжает“, „{who} сам постачає“, hi „देता है“/„रुका“,
  ar „يزوّد وحده“, „ابنِ آخر“, „يصنع“, „انتظر“) rephrased with adverbs, verbal nouns or an explicit
  „building“ noun.
- **Goods agreement.** Goods are bare nouns of mixed gender/number: ru/uk „его“, hi „पहुँचा/बनेगा“, ar
  „ينتجه“ now refer to an explicit „товар / माल / البضاعة“ or use a neutral construction; ko „{goodGen}이“
  wrong after goods without batchim (판자, 맥주) → „{goodGen} 생산이“.
- **ko particles after placeholders:** `status.WAGON_DUE` „{wagon}가“ → „이(가)“ (wagon ids are numbers);
  `fix.paceHaulWhy` „{aim}을“ → „{aim}를“ ({aim} always ends in 개).
- **Idioms:** ru/uk „Склад под/під замком“ means „locked up“ → „у замка / біля замку“; ru/uk „марка“
  (postage stamp/currency) → „пограничье / порубіжжя“ (cf. sv/nl „gränsmark/grensmark“); ru/uk „Цены на дворе“
  (= outdoors) → „Цены торгового двора“; ru/uk „лёд/крига обходит/оминає“ (calque of „kra omija“) → „не
  закрывает“; hi „सबसे देर से“ (= latest) → „सबसे लंबे समय से“; XIII title „floor“ → chain *link* in hi
  („कड़ी“) and ar („الحلقة“); ja/ru/uk kept („段“, „ярус“ read naturally).
- **Term consistency inside each language:** rank (ru „ранг“, ja „位階“, ko „등급“, hi „दर्जा“), rank-4 name in
  `chron.stronghold_rank.s` (ja 城塞, ko 성읍, zh 城寨, ar مدينة حصينة), renown (hi ख्याति, ar الصيت), seal (ja 封印),
  pace (zh 速度, hi गति), stall (ko 정체, zh 停滞), goods (hi माल), workshop (hi कार्यशाला).
- **Register:** ja fix explanations and cause fragments in plain form like the rest of `fix.*`/`cause.*`; ko
  `fix.growWhy*` in polite form like the other fix texts; ru cause/fix lines in „ты“ like „купи повозку“.
- **Punctuation:** zh full-width „——“ without spaces; hi „।“ instead of „.“ on the moderator screen and the
  invalid-fixture notice; ru/uk missing dash in „проданный товар — это …“.
- **Redundant „(Kup wóz, −30)“** in `end.act.wagon` removed in all seven (the Latin pass did the same).
- **Arabic RTL (order 100):** FSI/PDI isolation added where the string reaches the DOM through plain `t()`
  (`fix.pickupDue` {time}, `cause.paceCastle*` {late}, „+{silver}“ in `events.order.done`, „(−30)“ in
  `end.act.wagon`); see `docs/research/gpt-rtl-ar.md`.

Lengths: CJK and Korean strings are 0.2–0.9× en; ru/uk over-length kept where the natural term has no shorter
form (ru `events.order.t.fair_tables` 1.55, uk 1.55, `why.more` ru 2.27 / uk 1.92 — „промежуточное звено“,
`menu.help` „Справка“ 7 chars). Shortened: ru `pin.worst` 1.50 → 1.17, ru/uk `playtest.copy` 1.81/1.69 → 1.24/1.17.
