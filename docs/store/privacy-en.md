# Privacy Policy — MARCHIA: Roads of the Kingdom

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game **MARCHIA: Roads of the Kingdom** (the “game”), offered by the publisher identified on its Google Play or App Store listing. For privacy enquiries, contact **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.

## 1. Data collected by the game

The game requires no account, login or personal details. We do not collect or transmit names, email addresses, location, advertising identifiers, gameplay histories or device identifiers from the game. We do not use usage analytics, profiling or tracking. There are no ads or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Local game saves

Game progress, map state, resources and save information are stored **only on your device**, using the app's **IndexedDB** database. We do not upload game saves to a server and do not provide an account-based sync or cloud-save service.

You can remove local saves by clearing the app's storage or deleting the app from the device. Simply offloading the app may leave its data intact. Backups made by your operating system, if any, are governed by your device settings and the operating-system provider's terms.

## 3. One-time full-version purchase

Additional content may be unlocked through a one-time purchase in Google Play or the App Store. The relevant store operator handles payment transactions under its own privacy terms. The game does not collect payment-card details. The store may independently process purchase and account information.

## 4. Sharing and security

We do not share gameplay data with third parties because it never leaves the device. The game does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. Security of local saves also depends on your device and operating-system safeguards.

## 5. Voluntary contact

If you choose to email our contact address, we receive your email address and any information you provide. We use it only to respond or handle your request, based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the matter or as required by law. Email correspondence is separate from gameplay; the game does not automatically send such messages.

You may request access to, correction or erasure of personal information provided in correspondence, or restriction of processing, and may object to processing where the GDPR provides these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If we introduce online services, analytics or other features that change how data is handled, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before those features are released.
