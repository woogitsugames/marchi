## Name
Marchia: Roads of the Kingdom

## Subtitle
Find the cause of every stall

## Promotional Text
Every loaded wagon shows its cargo, and one tap explains a stall. Take on 5–12 minute castle contracts or build trade routes at your own pace.

## Description

The castle needs planks and bread. The sawmill is busy and the mill is grinding grain, but that doesn't mean the delivery will arrive on time. In Marchia, goods don't simply appear in storage: a wagon must carry them along a connected road.

Your main task is to discover why production or transport has stopped. Tap a bakery, mill or wagon and open “Why is it stalled?”. Find out whether the problem is missing ingredients, a busy fleet, an unreachable destination or full storage. Then remove the actual bottleneck.

Each loaded wagon visibly carries its goods. You can also check where it's heading and when the delivery is expected. Lay dirt tracks and cobbled roads across hills and rivers, adding bridges where needed. Rain slows wagons on muddy tracks; narrow crossings can bring an entire route to a halt.

5–12 minute contracts challenge you to deliver specified goods to the castle before the deadline. Earn seals for efficient deliveries. For a more relaxed session, choose the free valley and plan routes without a contract deadline.

Free Valley is a relaxed town-building mode with three separate save slots. Grow your settlement through ranks from a hamlet to a capital. Unlock fishing, quarry stone for cobbled roads and stone bridges, burn charcoal, produce malt and brew beer — while keeping every chain supplied by wagons.

All six missions (I–VI) and Free Valley are free, with no ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, energy meter or paid advantages. Game saves remain on your device.

Marchia is a medieval logistics strategy game about routes, deliveries and finding the causes of stalls. What matters isn't how many buildings you have, but whether the goods actually arrive.
