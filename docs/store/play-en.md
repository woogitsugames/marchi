## Title
Marchia: Roads of the Kingdom

## Short description
See each wagon's cargo. Tap once to find the cause of a stall.

## Full description

In Marchia, goods must actually reach their destination. Wood goes from the woodcutter to the sawmill, grain reaches the mill, and flour has to arrive at the bakery. Placing buildings side by side isn't enough: without roads and wagons, work stops.

This is a game about diagnosing stalls. Tap a wagon or workshop to find out why it's waiting. Is the bakery out of flour? Is a delivery cut off from the road? Is a bridge blocked? The “Why is it stalled?” panel lets you follow the chain of causes to the point where the supply line broke.

Cargo is visible on every loaded wagon. Inspect its destination and estimated delivery time. Draw dirt tracks, lay cobblestones, bridge rivers, and keep logs, planks, grain, flour and bread moving. Rain turns dirt roads to mud, while a narrow bridge can hold up the whole route.

Castle contracts are short missions designed for 5–12 minute sessions. Deliver the requested goods before the deadline and earn seals for efficient work. Prefer a quieter pace? Build your transport network freely in a valley without a contract deadline.

Free Valley is a relaxed town builder with three independent save slots. Grow your settlement through ranks from a hamlet to a capital to unlock fishing, quarrying stone for cobbled roads and stone bridges, charcoal burning, malting and brewing beer. Every supply chain still needs real wagon deliveries.

Twelve castle missions (I–XII) cover bridge jams, river ice, staged deliveries, a small granary, fish transport, paving before a storm, timber disputes, charcoal, malt and beer. Tap the worst stall to see why it stopped.

Free Valley has three independent settlement ledgers, ranks from hamlet to capital, seasons and a market. Royal orders, seasonal fairs and a travelling merchant are optional events.

Five key features:
- One-tap explanations for stalled buildings and wagons, including the chain of causes.
- Visible wagon cargo, destinations and estimated arrival times.
- Dirt tracks, cobbled roads and bridges that shape actual traffic.
- 5–12 minute contracts plus an open-ended valley without deadlines.
- Physical deliveries of logs, planks, grain, flour and bread — no instant teleporting goods.

All twelve missions (I–XII) and Free Valley are free. No ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid advantages or artificial waits. Play fully offline with no internet access. Saves stay on the device and may be transferable through system backups.
